How the conversion works

Lower the temperature

Air fryers use rapid hot air circulation, so we typically reduce temperature by 20–30°F (10–15°C) compared to conventional ovens.

Shorten the cooking time

Most recipes need 20–30% less time. We automatically adjust based on food type for optimal crispiness and doneness.

Check early and adjust

Every air fryer model is slightly different. Start checking food 5–10 minutes before the suggested time and shake/flip as needed.

Air Fryer Conversion Guide for Frozen Foods

8 min read • Updated August 2026

Frozen foods are one of the most popular things people cook in an air fryer. They usually need less time and a slightly lower temperature than the oven instructions on the package.

General Rules for Frozen Foods

  • Reduce temperature by 10–25°F
  • Reduce time by 15–25%
  • Shake or flip halfway through cooking
  • Do not overcrowd the basket

Popular Frozen Food Conversions

French Fries

Package (oven): 425°F for 20–25 min → Air Fryer: 400°F for 15–20 min

Chicken Nuggets

Package: 400°F for 15–20 min → Air Fryer: 380°F for 10–14 min

Mozzarella Sticks

Package: 400°F for 10–12 min → Air Fryer: 380°F for 6–9 min

Fish Sticks

Package: 425°F for 15–18 min → Air Fryer: 400°F for 10–13 min

Frozen Pizza (personal size)

Package: 400°F for 15–20 min → Air Fryer: 370–380°F for 8–12 min

Chicken Wings (frozen)

Package: 400°F for 30–40 min → Air Fryer: 380°F for 20–25 min

Extra Tips

  • Most frozen foods do not need to be thawed first
  • Check a few minutes early — air fryers vary
  • For extra crispiness, spray lightly with oil
  • Cook in a single layer whenever possible

Best Air Fryer Temperature for Chicken

8 min read • Updated August 2026

Getting the temperature right is the difference between juicy, crispy chicken and dry, disappointing results. Here’s the exact air fryer temperature and time for every common cut of chicken.

Quick Answer: Best Temperatures

  • Chicken breast: 375°F (190°C)
  • Chicken thighs: 380°F (193°C)
  • Chicken wings: 400°F (204°C)
  • Drumsticks: 380–400°F

Always cook chicken to an internal temperature of 165°F (74°C).

Complete Air Fryer Chicken Temperature & Time Chart

Cut Air Fryer Temp Time Internal Temp
Boneless chicken breast (6–8 oz) 375°F 15–20 min 165°F
Bone-in chicken breast 370°F 25–30 min 165°F
Boneless chicken thighs 380°F 16–20 min 165–175°F
Bone-in chicken thighs 380°F 22–26 min 165–175°F
Chicken wings 400°F 18–24 min 165°F
Drumsticks 380–400°F 18–24 min 165–175°F
Chicken tenders / strips 375–400°F 10–14 min 165°F

Times assume a preheated air fryer and a single layer. Always flip or shake halfway through.

Why Temperature Matters

Air fryers cook with intense circulating hot air. Too high a temperature and the outside browns before the inside is done. Too low and you lose that crispy skin. The temperatures above give the best balance of browning and juiciness.

Pro Tips for Perfect Air Fryer Chicken

  • Pat the chicken dry before seasoning — moisture is the enemy of crispiness.
  • Lightly spray or brush with oil for better browning.
  • Don’t overcrowd the basket. Cook in batches if needed.
  • Use a meat thermometer. Color alone is not reliable.
  • Let the chicken rest 3–5 minutes after cooking so the juices redistribute.
  • For thicker breasts, pound them to even thickness so they cook evenly.

Final Checklist

  1. Preheat the air fryer (optional but recommended)
  2. Pat chicken dry and season
  3. Set the correct temperature from the chart
  4. Flip halfway
  5. Check internal temperature reaches 165°F
  6. Rest before serving

Follow these temperatures and tips and you’ll get juicy, crispy chicken every time.

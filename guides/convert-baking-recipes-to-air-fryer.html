How to Convert Baking Recipes to the Air Fryer

9 min read • Updated August 2026

Baking in the air fryer is possible, but it requires more careful adjustments than regular cooking. Cakes, cookies, brownies, and muffins can turn out great when you convert the recipe correctly.

General Rules for Baking Conversions

  • Reduce the temperature by 25°F (15°C)
  • Reduce the baking time by 20–25%
  • Use smaller pans or ramekins that fit in your air fryer basket
  • Check for doneness earlier than the original recipe suggests

Common Baking Conversions

Cookies

Oven: 350°F for 10–12 minutes → Air Fryer: 325°F for 7–9 minutes

Brownies

Oven: 350°F for 25–30 minutes → Air Fryer: 320–325°F for 18–22 minutes

Muffins / Cupcakes

Oven: 350–375°F for 18–22 minutes → Air Fryer: 320–350°F for 12–16 minutes

Cakes (small)

Oven: 350°F for 30–35 minutes → Air Fryer: 320°F for 20–25 minutes (use a small cake pan)

Important Baking Tips

  • Avoid overfilling the pan — air needs to circulate
  • Cover with foil if the top is browning too quickly
  • Use the toothpick test to check doneness
  • Let baked goods cool slightly before removing them from the pan

When Baking Works Best in an Air Fryer

Smaller batches and individual portions generally work better than large cakes. Cookies, muffins, and brownies tend to give the most consistent results.

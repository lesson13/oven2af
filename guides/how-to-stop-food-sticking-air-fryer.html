How to Stop Food from Sticking in Your Air Fryer (Best Liners + Proven Tips)

Food sticking to the basket is one of the most common air fryer complaints. Here’s exactly how to fix it for good.

If you’ve ever pulled out chicken, fish, or fries only to find half of it glued to the basket, you’re not alone. Sticking is frustrating, ruins the food, and makes cleanup miserable.

The good news: it’s almost always preventable. Below are the methods that actually work, ranked from simplest to most effective, plus the best liners you can buy right now.

Why Food Sticks in an Air Fryer

Air fryers circulate hot air very aggressively. When food sits on a cold or dry surface, proteins and moisture can bond to the basket before a proper crust forms. Old residue, a worn non-stick coating, and aerosol cooking sprays make the problem worse.

7 Proven Ways to Stop Food from Sticking

1. Preheat the Air Fryer

Putting food into a cold basket is one of the biggest causes of sticking. Preheat for 3–5 minutes at the cooking temperature. The hot surface creates an instant sear that helps food release cleanly.

2. Use the Right Amount of Oil (the Right Way)

A light coating of oil is essential. Toss or brush the food itself with a high smoke-point oil (avocado, canola, or refined olive oil). Avoid spraying aerosol cooking sprays like Pam directly on the basket — the propellants and lecithin leave a sticky residue that ruins the non-stick coating over time.

3. Pat Food Completely Dry

Moisture is glue. Pat chicken, fish, and vegetables thoroughly with paper towels before seasoning or oiling.

4. Don’t Overcrowd the Basket

Food needs space for air to circulate. Overcrowding traps steam and causes steaming instead of crisping, which increases sticking.

5. Shake or Flip Halfway

For fries, nuggets, and vegetables, shake the basket halfway through. For larger items like chicken breasts or salmon, flip once.

6. Clean the Basket Regularly

Built-up grease and burnt bits act like glue. Wash the basket after every few uses with warm soapy water and a soft sponge. Avoid abrasive scrubbers that damage the coating.

7. Use Air Fryer Liners (The Easiest Fix)

Liners create a physical barrier between the food and the basket. They are the single most effective solution for sticky foods like fish, eggs, saucy chicken, or anything with cheese.

Quick Reference: Anti-Sticking Checklist

  • Don’t overcrowd
  • Use a liner for sticky or delicate foods
  • Shake or flip halfway
  • Clean the basket regularly

Bonus Tip: Convert Oven Recipes Correctly

Many sticking problems start with incorrect temperature and time. Air fryers cook faster and more intensely than ovens. Always reduce the temperature by about 25°F and the time by roughly 20%.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use regular parchment paper in an air fryer?

Yes, but it must be perforated or have holes so air can circulate. Never let loose paper touch the heating element.

Do silicone liners affect crispiness?

High-quality ones with low sides or ridges usually have little to no impact on crispiness when used correctly.

Why does my air fryer basket stick even when it’s non-stick?

The coating wears down over time, especially if abrasive cleaners or aerosol sprays were used. Liners solve this problem completely.

Crispy Air Fryer Chicken Breast from Oven Recipe

7 min read • Updated August 2026

Chicken breast is one of the most common recipes people convert from the oven to the air fryer. When done correctly, you get juicy meat on the inside and a nicely browned, slightly crispy exterior.

Original Oven Recipe

A typical oven recipe for chicken breast is:

  • Temperature: 400°F (200°C)
  • Time: 25 minutes

Air Fryer Conversion

Using the standard conversion rules, here are the recommended air fryer settings:

  • Temperature: 375°F (190°C)
  • Time: 18–20 minutes

Flip the chicken halfway through cooking for the best browning.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Pat the chicken breasts dry and season them well (salt, pepper, garlic powder, paprika, or your preferred seasoning).
  2. Lightly spray or brush with a small amount of oil for better browning.
  3. Preheat the air fryer to 375°F if your model requires it.
  4. Place the chicken in a single layer in the basket. Do not overcrowd.
  5. Cook for 9–10 minutes, then flip.
  6. Continue cooking for another 9–10 minutes, or until the internal temperature reaches 165°F (74°C).
  7. Let the chicken rest for 5 minutes before slicing.

Tips for Juicy Results

  • Do not cook thicker breasts for the full time without checking — use a meat thermometer when possible.
  • If the chicken is very thick, you may need a few extra minutes.
  • For extra crispiness, you can increase the temperature to 380–390°F for the last 2–3 minutes.
